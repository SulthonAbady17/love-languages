# Pricing Strategy

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-20

## Model: Free + One-Time Unlock

### Free tier
Everything that exists today:
- 25-statement love language assessment
- Ranked results with AI-generated personalised advice (Bahasa Indonesia)
- Permanent shareable result link (no account required)

### Paid unlock — Couples Comparison
**Price:** IDR 49,000–79,000 (~$4–7 USD) — one-time, no subscription.

What's included:
- Enter your partner's result link (or they take the assessment first)
- Side-by-side ranked profile comparison
- Combined AI-generated compatibility note highlighting where you align and where you differ

## Rationale

- **Free stays free** — the solo assessment is top-of-funnel. Every shared link is a distribution event; gating it would kill organic growth.
- **One-time, not subscription** — people take this once. A subscription has no recurring hook and would feel dishonest.
- **Couples Comparison is the natural upsell** — motivation to pay is highest immediately after completing the solo assessment, when the user wants to compare with their partner.
- **IDR 49,000–79,000 is the "just buy it" zone** — low enough to decide without friction, high enough to signal real value.

## What's Deferred

| Option | Why deferred |
|--------|--------------|
| PDF export | Adds complexity; shareable link covers the use case |
| Email follow-up series | Requires account/email capture; conflicts with privacy-first positioning |
| Subscription | No recurring value hook exists yet |
| Therapist / coach B2B | Different sales motion; revisit if consumer traction proves the model |

## Open Questions

- **Exact price point:** IDR 49,000 vs 79,000 — run an A/B test once traffic exists.
- **Partner flow:** Does the partner need to have taken the assessment first, or can the paying user enter a result link directly? (UX decision with pricing implications — a guided "invite your partner" flow may increase conversion.)
- **Payment provider:** Needs a provider that supports IDR and is low-friction for Indonesian consumers (e.g. Midtrans, Xendit).
